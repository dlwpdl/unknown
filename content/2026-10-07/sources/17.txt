Louise McKinney Riverfront Park
Main page content begins here
Welcome to the River Valley’s front door! Adjacent to downtown Edmonton and immediately south of the Edmonton Convention Centre, this park acts as a gateway between the urban downtown and the renowned River Valley parks and trails system. The park encompasses 12.9 hectares of land with a 500-metre breathtaking, unobstructed river view. The park is named after Louise Crummy McKinney, an advocate for women's rights and the first woman elected and sworn into the legislature in the British Empire.

Shumka Stage
What is Shumka? In Ukrainian, Shumka means “whirlwind” and it is used as the name of the Ukrainian dance group: Shumka. This weatherproof performance stage was named after the Ukrainian Shumka Dancers for their contribution to the Edmonton community. Annually, different groups utilize this space for various types of performances!
This covered stage may be booked for weddings, performances and other events. It is located near the Chinese Garden Ting which can be booked in conjunction with the stage for picturesque photo opportunities.

Parking
The parking lot located west directly behind the stage is managed by the Edmonton Convention Centre and is not available to be reserved for events. Limited parking is available onsite. Alternative parking is available in the downtown core which is easily accessible through the 100 Street Funicular or Grierson Hill.
A maximum of 5 stalls can be reserved for event bookings in the City of Edmonton-managed parking lot
Service roads along the park are for City staff and emergency vehicle use only.
