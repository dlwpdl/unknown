The Reuse Centre currently offers reusable items to Edmonton residents free of charge. Individuals and businesses can also donate items to be reused by others.
Edmonton’s Waste Reduction Network in Action
Date: Wednesday October 7, 2026
Time: 11am-1pm
Location: City of Edmonton Reuse Centre - 6835 83 Street NW
Join us and learn from Waste Reduction Network (WRN) members about how they’re reducing waste in Edmonton. Hear from Explore Edmonton on their experience successfully using reusables at festivals and events and from Reimagined Fabrics on how you can be part of the solution and help tackle one of the largest polluting industries–textiles.
The event will include a mending workshop where Reimagined Fabrics will help you mend an item you bring from home.

Reuse Centre Etiquette
Open All
Close All
We Nurture an Environment of Respect
Abusive and disrespectful behaviour towards staff and other customers will not be tolerated.
Allow 6 feet of room, around staff and other customers, in order for stocking and browsing to happen fairly and safely.
We Foster Sharing
We provide items for those who need it. Please only take what you need.
Access to the bins needs to be maintained at all times. To ensure fairness and safety among all patrons, do not remain around bins unnecessarily.
Reuse Centre Attendants reserve the right to ask patrons who violate these rules to change their behaviour.
