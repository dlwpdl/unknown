Park features - Bowness Park
Wading Pool
Bowness Park offers both a Wading pool at the east end. Access this amenity by parking in the main parking lot and then take a short walk east. Wading pools are typically open from mid-June until fall (weather dependent) and are operated by The City. They are un-guarded facilities open from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. Aquatic wheelchairs are now available at this spray park.
