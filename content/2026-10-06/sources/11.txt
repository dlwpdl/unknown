With its 97 hectares of forest lands, clearings and a 1.1 km lake, Parc Angrignon also has playgrounds and a dog park.
Description
In addition, the park was once home to a small farm and then a “fun fort” for children. Both are now closed. During winter, the park is the perfect place to practice snowshoeing and cross-country skiing, or work on your skating step.
