The Kitchen
Get ready to get your sauté, season and simmer on with The Kitchen!
The Kitchen, inside our Stanley A. Milner Library,
will offer
learning opportunities related to health, nutrition and food literacy.
Check out our 2,100 square feet of space, its features, equipment, services in-person or sign up for interactive online entry-level classes to learn from home.

Services & Equipment
Equipment
Are you ready to
learn, create and explore through cooking and baking? With a hybrid of commercial and home kitchen equipment, The Kitchen is perfect for all your cooking needs
. With commercial grade ovens, state of the art ventilation systems to keep you safe, and traditional at-home appliances like stand mixers, you’ll have the chance to experience it all!
Workstations
Our workstations are equipped with many gadgets, such as commercial ovens
and
induction cook tops
to accommodate
up to 36
participants and
a spacious
scullery
for cleaning with open shelving for storing materials.
Community Partnerships
Community groups, partners and other external groups will be able to rent The Kitchen for their own use based on availability. More details will be available at a later time.
Cooking for a Cause
Join
Cooking for a Cause
and embark on a culinary journey that supports the Edmonton Public Library (EPL). Enhance your cooking skills with an interactive kitchen session, contributing to various EPL initiatives while enjoying a premium, seasonal menu. Share this memorable experience with friends, family, or colleagues, and make a positive impact in your community.

click here

EPL Kitchen Coordinator
Our Kitchen Coordinator Zofia Trebaczkiewicz
will run and organize some virtual and in-person classes (when available).
Zofia is a chef with over 20 years of restaurant and catering experience. She has owned and operated several restaurants in Edmonton (Café Mosaics, Two Rooms Café, Flavours Modern Bistro). She has worked most recently as a Library Assistant at EPL and continued to be involved in the hospitality industry with small and large consulting projects, like the Prairie Bistro and Bakery, and Centre Polonia Restaurant Concept.

Frequently Asked Questions
FAQs
Where is The Kitchen located?
The Kitchen is located on
the
south east
corner of the second floor of the Stanley A. Milner
Library,
and
is only accessible through the library entrances. The Kitchen’s windows overlook Centennial Plaza
and
the Citadel Theatre.
When are new in-person Kitchen classes posted on your website?
Kitchen classes are posted on our website throughout the week. Check
Kitchen classes and events
often to discover new classes!
What is the goal of a kitchen in a library space?
Our goal is to
create
learning opportunities to help increase knowledge and grow interest
in
food literacy and nutrition
, and
to
teach skills that would be transferable into a home kitchen environment. A variety of cooking, baking and food related courses
at entry and intermediate level
s
will be offered
in The Kitchen
(either directly through the
Library
or through partner organizations)
.
How do I register for in-person Kitchen classes?
To register for in-person Kitchen classes and events, simply navigate to
Kitchen classes and events
. Once you find a class you’d like to attend, click on the class title to see more information. In the top right corner, you’ll find the link to register. Clicking on the link will open Zeffy, where you can register and pay for the class.
What types of activities will take place?
The Kitchen hosts a variety of activities including basic and intermediate classes, meal preparation workshops, nutrition workshops, knife skills classes, and a variety of demonstrations. Learn more about
Kitchen classes and events
.
