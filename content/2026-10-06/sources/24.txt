Your Library is about much more than just books!
Borrow a musical instrument from Memorial Park Library! Or take a free music workshop or attend an event.
Place a hold on an instrument and select Memorial Park Library as your hold pickup location. Instruments must be borrowed with a library card verified with photo ID and be returned to Memorial Park Library.

Borrowing Basics
Remember these details when signing out your instrument:
You’ll receive an email or phone call once the instrument is ready for pickup.
You have seven days to pick the instrument up from the Memorial Park Library.
A Library card verified with photo ID is needed to sign out an instrument.
You can borrow one instrument at a time.
You can have one instrument hold per card at any given time.
The loan period for an instrument is three weeks. If no one else has placed a hold on the instrument, you can renew it once, and keep the instrument for a total of six weeks.
If you lose or damage an instrument or its accessories, you may be charged a replacement or repair fee.

Need a place to practice?
Memorial Park Library has a free music practice room. Our practice space can comfortably accommodate two musicians and you can book the practice room twice per week, up to two hours each time.
The room has an upright double bass, donated by k.d. lang, an electric piano, electric drum kit, and a guitar.
