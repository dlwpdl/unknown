Think Montréal streets are cool? Just wait ‘til you see what’s underneath! Explore Montréal’s famed
Underground City
, a maze of shops, museums, attractions and more located right under the streets of downtown Montréal
. Linking metro stations to shopping plazas for over 33 kilometres (about 20.5 miles), the
RESO
underground pedestrian network — as it’s officially dubbed — is a lifesaver when you want to stroll, shop and eat away from the hot blazing sun or the freezing snow, but it’s also a go-to destination for the whole family anytime.

How to start your Underground City adventure
If you want a full-fledged professional tour of the Underground City, call upon the experts at
MTL Detours
and enjoy their fun and fact-packed
RESO Underground City + Downtown Montreal
walking tour. Otherwise, going it alone is a breeze.
Complexe Desjardins
is one of the easternmost starting points for the RESO network, smack bang in the middle of
Quartier des spectacles
, right across from
Place des Festivals
. Find the
RESO
signs throughout the Underground City to figure out where you’re heading on the underground pedestrian network, and explore the whole 20+ miles for lots more shopping, movies, activities and art.

Where to eat in the Underground City
When you want a break from all the shopping, there are amazing food options.
The
Centre Eaton de Montréal
is home to
Time Out Market
, a larger-than-life food hall that brings together different counters by some of the city’s best and most famous restaurants (including Le Red Tiger and Moleskine).
Place Ville Marie
also has a beautiful food hall,
Le Cathcart
, where you can people-watch in the amazing indoor-outdoor space while feasting on next-level burritos, poke bowls, Korean fried chicken or Montréal bagels and lox.
Nearby at the
Fairmont The Queen Elizabeth
hotel, conveniently connected to the city’s central train station, you’ll find the amazing
Marché Artisans
, where you can get food to go or enjoy a selection of meals from various stations, or sit down for a gastronomic experience at the stylish
Rosélys Restaurant
.
All the malls connected by the underground pedestrian network boast
their own food courts
, as well.

© Eva Blue - Tourisme Montréal
Cultural attractions connected to the network
When you want a break from all the shopping, wining and dining, there’s entertainment galore, all linked through the underground pedestrian network.
🎭 Head to the
Place des Arts
for cutting edge classical music, theatre, world sounds and dance
🖼️ Visit the
Musée d’art contemporain de Montréal
for the latest in contemporary art
🗿 Explore the annual
Art Souterrain
festival, a new collection of art pieces by local and international artists every edition
📍Discover the public art network maps and self-guided tours on the
Art Public Montréal
website too; many are reachable via the Underground City
🍿 Take in a movie at the
Cinéma Banque Scotia Montréal
🎳 Have a ball at arcade games, bowling and virtual reality experiences at
PLAYBOX Center
🌊 Get fully awash in audiovisual environments at
OASIS immersion
within the Palais des congrès de Montréal
There’s no doubt about it: Montréal’s Underground City is a climate-controlled microcosm of the city’s downtown core and all its riches. It’s packed with sights, sounds and flavours that will entertain you and the whole family. Make it a whole day or part of your day, and experience the city like a local would.
