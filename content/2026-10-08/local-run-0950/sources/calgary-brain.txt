The Brainasium Outdoor Park
Train your brain in Spark’s year-round outdoor park
.
Neuroscientists know there is a connection between healthy brains and healthy bodies. Physical play helps develop skills like problem solving, spatial thinking and cooperation. So ready, set … run, jump, climb and spin in the Brainasium Outdoor Park.
The Brainasium Outdoor Park is included with a Spark Membership or General Admission, and is open all year-round!
