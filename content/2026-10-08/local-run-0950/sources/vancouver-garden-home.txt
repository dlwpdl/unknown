Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Classical Chinese Garden is a registered museum and one of Vancouver’s top tourist attractions. It is a unique venue for cultural programming and events, including guided tours, concerts, festivals, exhibitions, receptions, and educational programs. First opened to the public in 1986, this Ming Dynasty-style garden-home is the first among its kind to have been built outside of China, and continues to be unique among the world as the result of the joint collaborative effort of community members, the Canadian Government, and the People’s Republic of China. As a registered not-for-profit all Garden revenue goes toward the fulfillment of our mission to bring together cultures and communities through educational and public programs.

Clay in the Classical Chinese Garden
What can a simple clay roof tile tell us about geology, craftsmanship, Chinese culture, and the art of the classical garden?
Join clay artisan and Garden docent
Colin Doerr
for an engaging exploration of clay and its remarkable role at the Garden. Combining a guided Garden tour, illustrated discussion and hands-on clay workshop, this experience invites participants to discover some of the Garden’s most easily overlooked—and fascinating—architectural and cultural details.
Working with stoneware and simple forming and impression techniques, each participant will create a clay piece inspired by the Garden’s roof and water-drop tiles. No previous ceramic experience is required.
Clay in the Classical Chinese Garden
is an invitation to look at the Garden differently—to follow clay from earth, to craftsperson, to kiln, to architecture, and to discover how a seemingly simple material can connect
nature, technology, craftsmanship, culture and art
.

Gongs at the Garden
On June 8 and 9, join us for Gongs in the Garden, an indoor-outdoor evening of sound and stillness.
Move through the paths, courtyards, and quiet spaces at your own pace before settling into the Hall of One Hundred Rivers for a 75-minute gong experience led by Aravind Vanchesan of Theta Space.
This is a deeply restorative, lying-down experience with no effort, movement, or prior meditation experience required. Guests will be guided through grounding, intention setting, breathwork, and a full gong journey designed to support deep rest and inner stillness.
The session features large gongs, including two 38-inch Sun Gongs, creating a layered resonance that many experience as spacious, timeless, and deeply calming.

A Message to Our Guests:
In order to ensure that we protect the Garden’s tranquil environment and provide every visitor with a safe and respectful experience, please note our on-site policies:
During our Special Events (Lunar New Year, Mid-Autumn Festival, Concert Series), guests are welcome to wear traditional or themed attire to maintain a comfortable atmosphere for all visitors. Photography is permitted with handheld equipment only.
Whether with a professional photographer or simply a friend, staged photo shoots are not permitted during the Garden’s public opening hours. Examples include: engagement photos, wedding photos, prom photos, senior or graduation photos, and posed portrait photos in costumes or special attire. For events such as these, find more information
here
.
Tripods, selfie sticks, drones, lighting equipment, or stabilizers are not permitted.
The Garden reserves the right to refuse admissions or request for visitors to leave if they fail to comply with our policies.
