Human
A Family Activity Where You Can Explore What We Are and What We’ll Become!
You are the result of millions upon millions of transformations. Come experience two interactive zones that help you understand your place along human evolution, the many things that make you what you are, and what humans might become in the future. Ideal for children and teens!
BUY YOUR TICKETS
The Ever-Transforming Human Body
Thanks to a series of interactive activities, come explore why and how the body undergoes a transformation at every stage of life. Slip into the skin of another human and get an extraordinary view of the beauty and wonder of the body.
Research and dig like an archeologist
Explore the body on an operating table
Experiment with moving, climbing, touching, and cycling
Compare your skeleton with those of others
Experience the sensation of pregnancy
See how much you’ve changed since you were born
Discover how the human body has evolved over millennia
The Future is Now
What if we pushed the boundaries of the human body? Try different scientific activities and explore technologies that are on the verge of changing our lives.
Play with a robot arm
Take part in futuristic transformations involving grafts, implants, stem cells…
Test out human-machine interfaces
Imagine the future of the human body using modelling tools
Ponder how far we’ll go to improve ourselves as humans
