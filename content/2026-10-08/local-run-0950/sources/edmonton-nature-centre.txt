Membership Terms and Conditions
A purchased membership is non-refundable and non-transferable
There are no extensions or holds available for any John Janzen Nature Centre passes
All visitors and users of recreation and leisure centres, and attractions, are required to follow posted Facility Use Safety Guidelines, including being respectful of people and property
Annual memberships can be cancelled at any time and the unused duration of the membership will be refunded back to you
Continuous monthly memberships can be cancelled before the 15th of every month without any fees
Annual Membership Benefits
Access to a year of unlimited visits during regular public hours (with a timed ticket).
Every visit offers a chance to connect with nature. The newly renovated facility is located in the heart of Edmonton's River Valley, a perfect natural landscape rich with wildlife and their habitats.
Along with your regular membership, you will receive 10% off regularly priced items in the John Janzen Nature Centre gift shop. To receive this discount, please advise our front desk staff of your membership to our facility.

Amenities
Food Services
Snack and beverage vending machines are available on site.
Picnic and Lunch Locations
We have many outdoor locations for having a snack or lunch. Picnic tables are located in front of the facility and are first-come, first-served and not reservable.
Our “Nest” is a small seating area where families can have their snacks. Bright rainbows and interactive additions make it a fun place to take a break.
Please help us keep our facility and grounds clean and free of litter. Use the garbage, green compost, and blue recycling bins located inside and outside. We are committed to be nature friendly, so when using the Birch Trail, please do not leave litter behind.
Gift Shop
Our Gift Shop provides a great selection of unique gifts and souvenirs. Find books, toys and more for science and nature lovers of all ages.
Note:
You do not need to pay facility admission to visit the gift shop.
Find the perfect gift or combine your visit with a stop at the Tegler Discovery Zone and Nature Centre.
Accessibility
Rating: Good
Good: partial accessibility
The John Janzen Nature Centre is an inclusive space and is committed to enabling the best possible access to its exhibits.
Nature Walk Trails
The Birch Tree and River Loop Trails have sections which may be challenging for those with limited mobility.
Accessible parking
Accessible nature exhibits
Accessible washrooms
The Tegler Discovery Zone and beehive are fully accessible
We offer an accessible washroom at the entrance and ramps in the Tegler Discovery Zone. A lift is available to access the classrooms.
Stroller Access
The John Janzen Nature Centre is a family-friendly space. We welcome strollers and have stroller parking by the Guest Services desk.
Sensory Accessibility
The John Janzen Nature Centre has partnered with
KultureCity
® to enhance our ability to assist and accommodate guests with sensory needs.
KultureCity® Sensory bags
KultureCity® Sensory bags contain special KCVIP badges, feeling thermometer, fidget tool, and noise-cancelling headphones.
These are available for checkout (at no cost by leaving an ID) at Guest Services located at the front entrance.
Weighted Lap Pads
Weighted Lap Pads are available upon request (at no cost by leaving an ID) at Guest Services located at the front entrance.
Quiet Areas
Quiet areas on dedicated days. Please ask staff if there is a quiet area if it is required.
Headphone Zones
Headphone Zones are located at the Tegler Discovery Zone, exhibit room and the Nest where you can eat.
