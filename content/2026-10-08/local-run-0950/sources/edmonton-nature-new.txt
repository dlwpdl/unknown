Whitemud Park is located just off Fox Drive between
Fort Edmonton Park
, the
John Janzen Nature Centre
,
kihcihkaw askî
and
Whitemud Equine Centre
. Picnic sites, multi-use trails and a well-utilized toboggan hill in the winter are key amenities for this park. It is also home to the
Alfred H. Savage Centre
available for year-round bookings.
