Special Thanks to our partners, sponsors, and supporters
The Alberta Aviation Museum is dedicated to keeping Edmonton’s rich aviation history alive. Our historic World War II hangar has more than 30 aircraft on display, a restoration area, gift shop, simulators, and a large events facility.
