    On Now

    Meet SUE The T. rex, the most complete, best-preserved Tyrannosaurus rex ever found.

    Watch T.REX in the Infinity Dome and travel back to the age of the tyrant lizard king in this giant-screen adventure.

    SUE: The T. rex Experience was organized by the Field Museum and is part of the Griffin Dinosaur Experience, made possible by the generous support of the Kenneth C. Griffin Charitable Fund.

    This fearsome fossil is ready to eat, er, meet you.

    Indigenous Science Night
