Fabrik² – Creative Challenges
Looking for things to do in Montréal? Dive into indoor activities inspired by the city’s biggest landmarks! The Montréal Science Centre celebrates its very own Montreality with four new creative challenges. Imagine and invent solutions to overcome technological challenges and build them using real tools and all sorts of different materials.
BUY YOUR TICKETS
Fabrik
2
is open on Saturdays and Sundays from noon to 5 p.m. On weekdays, mornings are reserved for school groups. Afternoon opening hours vary: please check with our call center or at the ticket office on the day of your visit.
C is for Challenge, and M is for Montreal!
The Metro
challenge is about designing an invention that can fly up an air tube and come out at the top.
The
Ville-Marie
challenge, inspired by Montreal’s original name, is about building a level structure that has specific dimensions. This highly interactive activity involves using motors, LEDs and circuit switches.
The Mount
challenge, an ode to Mount Royal, is about building a prototype that can travel from the top of a hill and stop in a specific area. Careful though! Road conditions change depending on whether it’s summer or winter.
The River
challenge, inspired by the St. Lawrence River, is about making a device that can float between the water surface and the bottom of the tank for at least 10 seconds.
A Tinkering-Based Experience
Fabrik
2
is a tinkering-based experience. Tinkering is a learning approach designed to:
Stir creativity and innovative thinking with challenges that can be solved in a variety of ways
Encourage collaboration and knowledge sharing
Foster knowledge acquisition and the cross-pollination of ideas through experimentation, all inside a non-competitive setting
Focus on the process rather than the end result
Turn mistakes into a source of inspiration and an opportunity to learn
Offer visitors a fun and highly engaging environment
Drop by the Dep
The Fabrik
2
space features a whole new area! Whether you’re bursting with ideas or in need of inspiration, no problem! Head over to the dep! Find different types of tools and materials to help you bring your inventions to life. Don't be shy and dig around for what you need with the help of an educator!

Visitors with special needs
Fabrik
2
is an accessible exhibition that particularly appeals to visitors with autism spectrum disorders, intellectual disabilities or reduced mobility. All challenges are designed to meet the needs of these visitors thanks to a multisensory approach and a series of adaptations. To learn more, consult the Science Centre’s permanent exhibitions visit
planning guide
, developed for those accompanying visitors with special needs.
