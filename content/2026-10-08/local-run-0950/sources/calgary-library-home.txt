Featured Programs
September 6 – October 26 | Multiple Locations
Math Quest
Drop in and explore the Library while completing fun numeracy challenges that support what you learn in school. Parents and caregivers are invited to stay and play.
Village Square Library | September 9 – October 21 | 2:30 pm
Words and Wiggles
Learn new ideas for play by exploring all five senses through movement, play, story, and song.
Saddletowne Library | September 14 - October 19 | 1:00 pm
Advanced English Practice
Enhance your English conversation skills and build your confidence in communication by joining this drop-in program. A strong intermediate level of English is required to participate.
Central Library | September 26 - October 21 | 1:00 pm
French Conversation Practice
Join us to practice your beginner to intermediate French skills in a welcoming and informal environment.
Online | September 28 and October 19 | 7:00 pm
Drop-In Teen Writing Cafe
Join a community of young writers to explore new ideas, spark creativity, and enjoy dedicated time to write and connect. For ages 13 – 17.
Central Library | October 8 | 6:00 pm
National Depression Screening Day: Mental Health Care for Turbulent Times
It's easy to feel helpless in the face of global challenges. Stressful world events like climate change, financial crises, political unrest, and information overload can have a detrimental impact on our mental health.
Central Library | October 8 | 7:00 pm
1914's 'Wonder Well' and the Birth of Alberta's Petroleum Culture
How did a single well change Calgary and shape Alberta’s future? Join historian Dr. Paul Chastko to explore the story of the 1914 Dingman well, known as the “Wonder Well,” and the oil boom that followed.
Central Library | October 10 | 12:00 pm
All Aboard the Brain Train: Brain Health Awareness Week
Challenge your brain with interactive games, learn about mental health conditions like ADHD, meet service dogs, build your own Lego neuron, and try mental health exercises. Fun for the whole family!
Central Library | October 10 | 12:00 pm
National Depression Screening Day: "Inside Out 2"
Bring the whole family to learn healthy anxiety-reducing exercises and coping strategies from Calgary Counselling Centre experts, followed by a free screening of Disney Pixar's "Inside Out 2."
Central Library | October 11 | 1:00 pm
Author in Residence: Dialogue – A Practical Workshop for Screen & Play Writers
Another word for dialogue is conversation, but when conversation is used to help tell a story on stage there are tricks to make it more effective and pitfalls to avoid.
Central and Memorial Park Library | October 13 – 18
Wordfest Imaginairium
Imaginarium, Canada’s Liveliest Literary Festival, is back. Hear from over 50 authors and writers at storytelling and poetry events. Select programs are free when you register with your Library card!
Online | October 13 | 4:30 pm
Creative Explorers: Draw! Find Your Inner Artist
Tap into your artistic side and discover the fun and creativity in drawing! Gain confidence through guided drawing classes that will build your art vocabulary and expand your skill set, taking you from a blank page to a masterpiece you can be proud of.
Online | October 14 | 4:30 pm
STEM Explorers: With a Blast of Liquid Nitrogen
Have you ever wondered what it feels like to touch a cloud? Are you curious about the amazing science of the air that surrounds us? Then this sub-zero spectacular is for you!
Online | October 14 | 7:00 pm
Art Night In: Single Panel Comics
Popularized by newspaper comics like The Far Side, single-panel comics are an exciting format that challenges us to tell stories with only a single illustration and limited writing.
Sage Hill Library | October 15 | 1:00 pm
Seniors Drop-In Social Club: Conversation, Games & Activities (55+)
Join us to engage in conversation and make new friends in a comfortable, friendly environment while you enjoy a cup of coffee or tea, fun activities, games, topics and occasional speakers, supported by a Library Volunteer Host.
Central Library | October 15 | 6:00 pm
Job Searching Strategies for Refugees & Newcomers
Searching for a job in a new country can feel overwhelming, especially if you're only applying online. This interactive session will share practical tips on where to look for jobs, how to build your network, and other job search methods that can help you
Signal Hill | October 19 — 21 | 12:45 pm
Business Feasibility Study
Join the experts from Momentum Calgary. Held over 3 days, this program is suitable for anyone who has a clear business idea and may be wondering about the feasibility of their idea. Participants must attend all three days.
Online | October 20 | Multiple Times
Building Resilience Through Movement
This facilitator led program offers gentle mindfulness, calming movement, and simple physical activities to help you and your child build resilience, strengthen your bond, and support healthy development.
Central Library | October 21 | 7:00 pm
Archaeology in Focus: The Vissotzky Petroglyphs
Discover the Vissotzky Petroglyphs near Whitefish, Montana, with the Archaeological Society of Alberta. Explore more than 700 ancient carvings and learn what they reveal about late Archaic culture.
Central Library  | October 21 | 6:00 pm
Ideas in the City: Hail the Nocturnal
When the sun sets, another world awakens. Nighttime has inspired dreams and nightmares, creativity and rebellion, mystery and wonder.
Central | October 24  | 10:00 am
Kids’ BoucherCon 2026
Join us for a fun-filled day of learning from your favourite children's mystery writers! Learn about code cracking, cryptid hunting, how to write whodunnits, and more.
