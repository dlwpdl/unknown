Located along the North Saskatchewan River, the Edmonton Valley Zoo prioritizes animal welfare and community environmental responsibility.
Come and explore one of Edmonton's premier attractions!
The Edmonton Valley Zoo is home to more than
250 animals
, focused on conservation, and offers fun and education for the whole family.
Big things are happening at the Edmonton Valley Zoo!
We have started the Enclosure Renewal and Enhancement Project, which focuses on modernizing habitats for Lucy and our carnivores. You will encounter fenced-off zones and rerouted pathways. Some animals have been moved to allow for safe construction.

Conservation Carousel
Open:
During Zoo hours
Cost:
Free (donations to conservation efforts are accepted)
Location:
Meltwater Play
Take a spin on the conservation carousel! Each of the carousel animals represents an endangered animal that calls the zoo home. All proceeds will be donated to conservation programs supported by the Edmonton Valley Zoo. Riders must be under 50 inches (127centimetres) in height.

Guest and Animal Safety
For the safety of all our guests and animals, we do not allow the following:
Pets (with the exception of service dogs)
Service dogs must wear a vest and appropriate harness
Guests will be asked to provide the dog's Government of Alberta issued ID or a card showing they have been trained by an accredited school (such as
Dogs with Wings
and
Assistance Dogs International
)
Emotional Support Animals do not meet these requirements
Balloons
Bubbles
Balls and frisbees
Bikes, scooters, skateboards and rollerblades
