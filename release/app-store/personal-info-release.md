# Optional name and birth date

The next native build includes optional private name/date-of-birth entry at onboarding and in Settings. Members can correct or delete it; public profiles keep nicknames. Admin detail and age distribution derive completed years from the UTC calendar date and label the information self-reported. No paid verification service or new dependency was added.

Before distributing the next binary, review the App Store/Google Play privacy declarations for name and date-of-birth collection, account association, account/support/safety use, access/deletion and tracking-not-used answers. Check name/date keyboard entry and consent accessibility on a device. Existing build 11 does not contain these controls or the earlier new GPS module. Kakao name/birthday/birthyear permissions remain unapproved; no new scopes are enabled.
