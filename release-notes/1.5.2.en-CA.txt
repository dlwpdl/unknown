The map now does less work when you drag it or tap a pin. Map images you’ve loaded stay saved on your phone, and the place list downloads once a day.
Place pages have a new layout with the price range, hours and address up top, plus Website, Call, Instagram and Book buttons when the place has them.
Tap the pencil on a map card, or “Post about this place” on a place page, to start a post with that place already attached.
Adding a place to a post now opens the map with the same pins as the Map tab. Places picked this way show as a card on Home and on the post, and their name comes along in chat previews and shares.
New map filters: Breakfast, Karaoke, Speakeasy, Sports bar, Jazz, Happy hour, Ice cream and Matcha. Pins now show the kind of place, like Korean, rather than a situation like Lunch.
Businesses with a webshop now have an “Order from webshop” button on their map card. It shows the price comparison first.
Place and meetup photos now fill their frames, and street names on the map no longer cover place pins. We also fixed the sign-in sheet popping up by itself when you open the app without signing in.
